CORE PILLARS

WHAT STUDENTS CAN DO

A collaborative platform designed entirely around student peer learning and skill sharing.

Learn Skills

Master programming languages, design tools, and academic topics directly from campus peers who have already built tangible projects.

Teach Skills

Reinforce your own knowledge by mentoring fellow students. Share your applied proficiency in software, multimedia, or coursework.

Exchange Skills

STEP-BY-STEP

HOW THE PLATFORM WORKS

A clear, simple 4-step path from creating your profile to sharing knowledge.

01
Set Up Profile

Register with your credentials and specify the skills you can teach alongside the skills you want to learn.

02
Discover Matches

Search peer offerings by category and find students ready for reciprocal exchange in your department or college.

03
Propose Exchange

Send a structured proposal detailing the skill offered, skill wanted, preferred mode (online/in-person), and note.

04
Collaborate & Learn

Once accepted, coordinate via real-time chat, conduct mutual study sessions, and complete your learning milestones.
