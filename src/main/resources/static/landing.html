03 / LEARNING MODES

You Decide How to Meet

Every exchange runs your way — online via Zoom, offline on campus, or purely over chat.

ONLINE

Online Zoom Sessions

Pick a date and time, and a real Zoom meeting is automatically created right in your exchange schedule. Host controls protect start links while learners receive instant join URLs.

OFFLINE

On-Campus Meetings

Meet face-to-face at the college library, computer labs, or department seminar rooms. Perfect for hands-on pair programming, hardware testing, and design critiques.

CHAT

In-Platform Chat

Coordinate session timings, ask doubt questions, share code snippets, and record shared session notes all within one dedicated conversation thread.
