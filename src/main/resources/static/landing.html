How Students Exchange Skills on SWAP

Three straightforward steps to start collaborating with classmates across departments.

01

List Your Skills

Add skills you can teach (e.g. Core Java, Python, Photoshop, Public Speaking) and what you want to learn.

02

Find Compatible Peers

Match with college students whose teaching offerings reciprocate what you need. Propose a balanced barter trade.

03

Coordinate & Learn

Chat in real time, share photos or notes, meet in campus labs or online, and earn verified skill badges for your profile.
