How Student Skill Exchange Works

Designed specifically for university students to collaborate and grow together.

1. Smart Compatibility Matching

Our explainable 5-factor algorithm matches your learning desires with peers offering those skills, prioritizing mutual two-way exchanges.

2. Integrated Chat & Modes

Discuss schedules and syllabi effortlessly. Pick your preferred medium: Online video meeting, campus offline meet, or built-in chat.

3. Admin Verified Badges

Upload certificates or project repositories. College admins audit submissions to grant prestigious Verified Skill Badges.
